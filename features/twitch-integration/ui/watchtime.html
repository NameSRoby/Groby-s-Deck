Watchtime & points

Track estimated time in live chat. Non-followers are excluded. Raids never change your chosen mode.

People outside the chosen mode earn no time or points. Saving the subscriber-only mode removes existing watchtime records without verified subscription history.

Automatic cleanup

Eligible watchtime and points stay indefinitely. When runtime history reaches its storage cap, the least recently active users are removed first, across accounts. Quotes and settings are protected.

Presence is sampled every five minutes. Eligible people earn 10 points per 10 tracked minutes. Follower checks use a bounded cache; a confirmed unfollow removes that person's tracking record.

Previous subscriptions

The app remembers verified followers' subscriptions from chat badges and current Twitch subscriptions. Twitch does not provide a complete list of expired subscriptions from before the app started tracking. An old subscription cannot be guessed.
